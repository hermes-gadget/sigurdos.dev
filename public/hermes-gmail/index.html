HERMES GMAIL (READ-ONLY)

A personal, single-user integration. Not a public service.

Hermes Gmail (read-only) is a private, personal-use integration that lets its owner's own AI assistant (run on the owner's own self-hosted hardware) read the owner's own Gmail account.

What it does

  • Connects to Gmail with read-only access (gmail.readonly), granted by the account owner.
  • Lets the owner search and read their own emails and threads through their private assistant.
  • It cannot send, delete or modify anything — read access is the only permission requested.

Who uses it

Exactly one user: the owner of the Gmail account and of this website. No other users, no accounts, no sign-ups.
